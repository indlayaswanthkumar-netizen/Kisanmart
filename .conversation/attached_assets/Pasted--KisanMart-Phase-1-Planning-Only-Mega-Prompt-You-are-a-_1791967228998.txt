## RLS SECURITY MATRIX

Document policies for every table.

Farmers:
- Read and update permitted fields of their own profiles.
- Manage their own addresses, carts, wishlists, and other private records.
- Read their own orders and related order information.
- Create reviews only for eligible purchases.
- Never access another customer's private data.

Sellers:
- Read and update their own seller profile where permitted.
- Manage only their own products and variants.
- Access orders and order items associated with their own sales only.
- Never access other sellers' private business information or unrelated customers' records.
- Never approve their own seller account or grant themselves elevated privileges.

Administrators:
- Use server-verified privileges and narrowly scoped administrative policies.
- Perform authorized moderation and management actions.
- Never trust role claims supplied by the browser.

Public users:
- Read active, published categories and products.
- Never read private addresses, carts, orders, seller documents, payments, or internal moderation data.

Review all foreign-key relationships for indirect information leaks. RLS must be enabled on every application table, and all policies must be explicitly documented.

Do not assume that enabling RLS automatically protects service-role operations. Service-role credentials bypass normal RLS and must remain server-side.

## STORAGE

Plan two buckets:

1. product-images
   - Public read only for published product assets.
   - Restricted uploads and updates for authorized sellers.
   - Validate file size, file type, and ownership.
   - Prevent arbitrary users from replacing another seller's images.

2. seller-documents
   - Private bucket.
   - No public read access.
   - Authorized seller uploads only for their own verification documents.
   - Administrative review through trusted, authorized access.
   - Use short-lived signed URLs when access is needed.

Do not expose license documents publicly.
